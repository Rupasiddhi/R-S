Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Rupasiddhi mobile application (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed as a Progressive Web App (PWA) wrapper using Trusted Web Activity (TWA) technology.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Rupasiddhi App is an educational application designed to provide Buddhist educational content. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not access your device's camera, microphone, or contacts.
  • We do not track your location.

2. Web Content and Third-Party Services

The App displays content hosted on our website (https://rupasiddhi.vercel.app). While using the App, standard web browsing data (such as IP address and browser type) may be processed by our hosting provider (Vercel) for security and performance purposes. However, this data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data at all, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
